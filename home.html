Why this exists

Every year, ASF RSU's departments produce a steady stream of records — reports from Sabbath School, plans of work from Ushering, programs from the Music Director, inventories from Welfare, and much more. Historically, this kind of paperwork has lived scattered across phones, group chats, and personal drives, easy to lose track of and hard to hand over when leadership changes each session. This portal exists to fix that: one place, organized the same way for every department, that outlasts any single leader's tenure.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your department account. Every department — from the Executive officers to each ministry — has its own login, so uploads are always tied to the right place.
  2. Upload into one of four folders. Monthly Reports, Plan of Work, Programs/Schedule, and Inventories — the same structure for every department, so nothing gets misplaced.
  3. Browse any department, any time. There's nothing confidential about department records here — any signed-in member can look through any department's files, and the Archive makes older documents searchable by name.

About ASF RSU

Adventist Student Fellowship (ASF), RSU, is a community of students committed to fellowship, service, and spiritual growth on campus. Across every department — from Sabbath School to Music, Ushering, Welfare, Sports, and beyond — members give their time to keep the fellowship running week to week.

This portal was built for that community specifically: a place where the day-to-day paperwork of running a student fellowship — reports, plans of work, programs, inventories — can be kept properly instead of lost between phones and group chats, and handed smoothly from one set of leaders to the next.
